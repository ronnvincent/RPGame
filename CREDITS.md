# Art and audio credits

Several packs used here are free but ask for attribution. This file is the
record of that, and should be reproduced somewhere the player can reach it
before the game is submitted or published.

## Maps

- **Parallax Forest Background (Seamless)** — Digital Moons
  <https://digitalmoons.itch.io/parallax-forest-background>
  Used for the Goblin Catacombs. Free for personal and commercial use, credit
  requested. Layer order follows the pack's own Read Me.

- **PolyStyle SideScroll Village Pack** — Kalponic Studio
  <https://kalponic-studio.itch.io/polystyle-sidescroll-village-pack>
  Used for the town: buildings, props, nature and ground tiles.

## Not currently used, downloaded as alternatives

- **Parallax Backgrounds: Caves** — Admurin
  <https://admurin.itch.io/parallax-backgrounds-caves>
  8 layers, 384x216. Free, commercial use allowed, no reselling.

- **Free Pixel Art Forest** — Eder Muniz
  <https://edermunizz.itch.io/free-pixel-art-forest>
  12 layers, 928x793. The bundled license.txt permits modification and
  commercial use with credit, and describes itself as closest to CC BY 4.0 -
  note that the itch.io page states CC BY-ND instead. Redistribution as an
  asset is prohibited either way.
